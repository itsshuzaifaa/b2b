AUTOMATED COMMUNICATIONS & VERIFICATION GUIDE

Final reviewed wording for platform implementation

SYSTEM OVERVIEW & LIFECYCLE TRIGGERS All B2B LINK accounts are unified wholesale accounts that can be used to both buy and sell products.

Newly registered accounts remain in Pending Verification status until the required business checks have been completed. Verification confirms that B2B LINK has carried out its membership checks; it does not guarantee a company, product or transaction.

This guide defines the approved automated email, in-app and internal admin messages used throughout registration, verification, onboarding and support. Automated customer-facing messages should come from a B2B LINK department rather than an individual staff member.
APPROVED DEPARTMENT NAMES
  • B2B LINK Support Team - General enquiries, welcome messages and account help.
  • B2B LINK Verification Team - Business verification, additional information requests and account reviews.
  • B2B LINK Membership Team - Membership plans, renewals and account-plan matters.
  • B2B LINK Trading Support - Trading Board and listing assistance.
SECTION 1: AUTOMATED EMAIL MESSAGES
1. Email Verification
Registration Trigger
Trigger Event: User submits the registration form
Recipient: Registered business email address
Subject: Verify Your Email Address - B2B LINK

Hello [Contact Name],

Welcome to B2B LINK.

To verify your email address and continue setting up your account, please enter the following verification code:

[Verification Code]
This code is valid for 15 minutes.

Alternatively, click the button below to verify your email address:

[Verify Email Address]

Kind regards,
B2B LINK Support Team

2. Business Verification Request
Action Required
Trigger Event: Sent after the business email address has been verified
Recipient: Registered customer contact email
Subject: Action Required: Complete Your B2B LINK Business Verification

Hello [Contact Name],

Thank you for registering [Company Name] with B2B LINK.

Your email address has been verified, and your account is now awaiting business verification.

Our Verification Team may check information including company registration details, VAT details where applicable, business contact information, company email/domain and bank account information.

If we require any additional information or supporting documents, we will contact you directly.

Your account will remain in Pending Verification status until the review has been completed.

Kind regards,
B2B LINK Verification Team

3. New Member Verification - Internal Admin Alert
Internal Admin Alert
Trigger Event: Generated automatically when a new company registers
Recipient: Internal B2B LINK administration / verification queue
Subject: New Member Verification Required - [Company Name]

A new company has registered with B2B LINK and is awaiting verification.

Company: [Company Name]
Contact: [Contact Name]
Email: [Email]
Telephone: [Phone]
Country: [Country]

Please review the company information in the Admin Panel and complete the required verification checks.

Current Status: Pending Verification

4. Account Verified
Verified Member
Trigger Event: Sent when the Verification Team activates the member account
Recipient: Customer business email address
Subject: Your B2B LINK Account Has Been Verified

Hello [Contact Name],

We are pleased to confirm that the business verification for [Company Name] has been completed.

Your account is now active and your company has Verified Member status on B2B LINK.

You can now access the features available under your membership, including:

  • Browse live BUY and SELL listings
  • Post and manage your own BUY and SELL requirements
  • Receive relevant Buyer-Seller Matches
  • Search for products and trading opportunities
  • Connect directly with other members

You can log in to your account to get started.

Welcome to B2B LINK.

Kind regards,
B2B LINK Verification Team

5. Account Under Review / Suspended
Account Notice
Trigger Event: Sent when additional verification information is required, or when an active account is placed under review
Recipient: Customer business email address
Subject: Update Regarding Your B2B LINK Account

Hello [Contact Name],

We are contacting you regarding the B2B LINK account for [Company Name].

Account Status: Under Review

We require additional information before we can complete the verification of your account.

Please reply to this email with any information requested by our Verification Team.

Once the required information has been received, we will continue our review and update you as soon as possible.

Kind regards,
B2B LINK Verification Team

If an already-active account is genuinely suspended, use: “Account Status: Suspended. Your B2B LINK account has been temporarily suspended while we review an account or verification matter. Please contact the B2B LINK Verification Team for further information.”

FINAL TERMINOLOGY STANDARD
Use Avoid / Replace
Verified Member Certified business / approved trader
Business Verification KYC, unless legally required for a specific process
BUY / SELL listing WTB / procurement request, unless intentionally used
B2B LINK Support Team Compliance Desk for ordinary customer support
Under Review Suspended, unless the account has actually been suspended
Buyer-Seller Match AI Match / Smart Match in customer-facing core terminology
Implementation note: Customer-facing automated communications should be sent from the appropriate B2B LINK department rather than a named individual, so messaging remains consistent if staff responsibilities change.
